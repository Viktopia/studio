How we work,
stated plainly.

Three rules we keep coming back to whenever there's a decision to make.

  1. Problem-first.

    We only build for problems we've actually run into. If we wouldn't use it ourselves on a slow Wednesday, we don't ship it.

  2. Open at the core.

    The source is open. The business is durable. We trust that good software and a fair commercial layer can — and should — coexist.

  3. Earn your keep.

    Bootstrapped, patient, profitable. We grow at the speed of customers who'd miss us if we vanished — not the speed of a fundraise.
